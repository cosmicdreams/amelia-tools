## Course description

This course is an introduction to how natural systems work and how human activity changes them. We study energy flow, the carbon and water cycles, biodiversity, climate change, pollution, and the policy choices societies make in response. The goal is for you to be able to read an environmental claim in the news, judge the evidence behind it, and explain the underlying science clearly in your own words.

## Learning goals

By the end of the term you should be able to:

1. Describe the major Earth systems and how energy and matter move through them.
2. Explain the scientific basis of climate change and evaluate proposed responses.
3. Read and interpret graphs and data tables from environmental measurements.
4. Write a clear, evidence-based argument about an environmental question.
